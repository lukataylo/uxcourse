## Summary (≤150 words)

Strongest signal comes from **Design Better** (Walter/Woolery) which has effectively become the canonical podcast for "designing for AI" — multiple deep episodes with Adobe, Perplexity, Google Search, ServiceNow, and the *Sentient Design* authors. **Lenny's Podcast + How I AI** dominate the practitioner-workflow angle (Jenny Wen, Dylan Field, Guillermo Rauch, Joel Unger, Gui Seiz). **NN/g UX Podcast** anchors the academic/heuristics view (Nielsen, Norman, Gibbons, Moran). **a16z** covers the dev-tools/IDE frontier (Cursor, Poolside). 

**Gaps to fill with custom interviews:**
- Mid-career IC designers actually using AI day-to-day (most public episodes feature VPs/founders).
- UX **researchers** on AI for synthesis, recruiting, and analysis — thin coverage beyond NN/g.
- Designers at non-AI-native enterprises (banks, healthcare, gov) navigating AI adoption.
- Critical/skeptical voices on AI design ethics beyond Don Norman.
- Design education perspectives (only Brooke Hopper's Parsons partnership surfaced).
- Non-US/EU designers — dataset skews heavily SF/NYC.
