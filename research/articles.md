## Summary (≈140 words)

The corpus is rich on **opinion essays** (NN/g, Smashing, UX Collective, A List Apart) and a strong **practitioner spine** from Brad Frost, Josh Clark, Maggie Appleton, Linus Lee, Julie Zhuo, Pavel Samsonov, and Jakob Nielsen, plus institutional voices (IDEO, frog, Figma, Pentagram, Intercom, John Maeda). Dominant themes cluster around: (1) taste, curation, and judgment becoming the differentiator; (2) the "design engineer / vibe design" role blur; (3) probabilistic, agentic, and generative UI as new design materials; (4) craft and design-systems as the constraint that makes AI useful; (5) career anxiety and the redefinition of "good."

**Obvious gaps** to fill next: Adobe XD/design blog interviews (mostly product marketing surfaced), Google Design's Medium publication (search returned third-party posts), Designer Fund / DesignerHangout content, The Pudding, Fast.ai blog, more first-person interviews with researchers (vs. designers), Amelia Wattenberger's own essay pages (her site indexes poorly via search), and Don Norman's primary writings on jnd.org. Also under-represented: non-Western voices, accessibility-specific AI essays beyond Gustafson, and enterprise/B2B design case studies.
