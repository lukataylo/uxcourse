## Summary

The dataset spans 35 high-signal talks, biased toward **Figma Config** (the densest single source of AI/design content, especially 2024–2025), **Big Medium / Josh Clark / Brad Frost** for the design-systems angle, and **independents like Maggie Appleton, Matt Webb, Pablo Stanley, and Ioana Teleanu** for product-design pattern work. The highest-signal talks are: Joel Lewenstein's *Beyond Agents* (Config 2025), Maggie Appleton on LLMs as composable reasoning engines (UX London 2024), Matt Webb's GenAI product landscape (UX London 2024 + essay), Josh Clark's *Sentient Design*, and the Phillip Maggs / Jordan Singer / Sam Whitmore Config 2024 trio.

**Gaps.** IxDA Interaction was cancelled (2024), so the IxDA tradition is missing in the dataset. **An Event Apart** ended in 2023, with no AI-era talks. **Web Summit, AIGA Design Conference 2024 (theme: Margins), Adobe MAX, WWDC, Meta Connect** lacked focused public AI-for-designers talks; their AI content was mostly product marketing or developer-facing. **UX Lisbon, UX Copenhagen, Clarity, Schema** have AI workshops/panels but few canonical video keynotes that survive as standalone artifacts. Strong opportunity area: a serious **conference talk on AI ethics & dark patterns for designers**, and on **AI for design research** (still mostly podcasts and essays, not stage talks).
