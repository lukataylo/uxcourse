## Summary (≈140 words)

I assembled ~34 substantive videos spanning 2019–2025, weighted heavily toward 2023–2025. Strongest coverage: Figma Config (2023/24/25) and Schema (2025), NN/g (Jakob Nielsen, Kate Moran, Sarah Gibbons, Don Norman), and practitioner deep-dives from Josh Clark, Brad Frost, Vitaly Friedman, Ioana Teleanu, Maggie Appleton, and Linus Lee. Awwwards (Zumbrunnen, Zhang), Adobe MAX 2024/25, and a Google Design Gemini case study round out the corporate angle. Recurring substantive themes: AI as design material (not feature), generative UI / per-user interfaces, agentic design systems, beyond-chat patterns, motion as AI's language, and the productivity-vs-replacement debate.

**Gaps to fill manually:** (1) Microsoft Design lacks a clean conference talk on YouTube — most material is blog/Behind-the-Design; (2) AJ&Smart has not produced a single canonical AI-talk video, only scattered shorts; (3) Mike Kus and Sarah Drasner have no recent AI-focused talks (Drasner's "Live and Machine Learn" 2018 is the closest); (4) DesignCourse (Gary Simon) and Flux Academy (Ran Segall) skew toward tutorials, excluded per "no listicles/beginner" filter; (5) Need to verify exact durations and upload dates by hand — YouTube blocked direct scraping via WebFetch.
